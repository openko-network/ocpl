# OCPL Brand

## Mark Concept
Open lock inside the chamber ring — a padlock silhouette with its shackle open (drawn as simple geometric paths, not clip art), in sky blue. Below the lock: a thin amber horizontal line (the protocol baseline / foundation). Fog dashes at the perimeter suggest the ring of participants. Reads "open + protected" — abstract geometric statement.

## Type Stack

- **Display:** Space Grotesk → IBM Plex Sans → system
- **Body:** IBM Plex Sans → Space Grotesk → system
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono

## Do / Don't

- **DO:** Use geometric, sparse lines
- **DO:** Keep the focal point slightly heavier
- **DON'T:** Use clip art or literal padlocks
- **DON'T:** Overcrowd the layout
